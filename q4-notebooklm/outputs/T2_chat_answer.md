Woolworths Group’s Scope 3 emissions disclosures and the requirements set out in **AASB S2 Climate-related Disclosures** compare across several key areas:

---

### **1. How Woolworths Group Discloses Scope 3 Emissions**

* **Value Chain Impact:** Scope 3 emissions account for **96%** of Woolworths Group’s total end-to-end value chain footprint (approximately 23 times greater than its combined Scope 1 and 2 emissions).
* **Inventory Structure & Split:** Using an F23 base year, Woolworths splits its Scope 3 footprint into two equal halves:
  * **50% Forest, Land, and Agriculture (FLAG):** Pre-farmgate agricultural and land-use emissions.
  * **50% Energy and Industrial:** Post-farmgate operational inputs such as electricity, gas, transport, packaging, services, and downstream waste.
* **Independent Targets (SBTi Validated):** Woolworths sets separate near-term and long-term absolute gross reduction targets against an F23 base year:
  * **Energy & Industrial Scope 3:** **-55% by F33** (near-term) and **-90% by F50** (long-term).
  * **FLAG Scope 3:** **-40% by F33** (near-term) and **-72% by F50** (long-term).
* **Supplier Engagement & Data Collection:** Woolworths reports that suppliers representing **28%** of its Scope 3 purchased goods and services emissions participated in its supply chain emissions program in F25. It also collaborates on digital tools (such as partnering with Ruminati) to simplify farm-level emissions baselining.
* **Reporting Structure:** Woolworths presents its primary climate-related financial disclosures (governance, strategy, metrics, and risk management) in its **Annual Report**, while its **Sustainability Report** acts as a complementary document tracking 2025 Plan progress.

---

### **2. What AASB S2 Requires for Scope 3 Emissions**

* **Mandatory Absolute Reporting:** Entities must disclose absolute gross Scope 3 GHG emissions generated during the reporting period.
* **15 GHG Protocol Categories:** AASB S2 explicitly mandates categorising Scope 3 emissions across the **15 categories** defined in the *GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Accounting and Reporting Standard (2011)*.
* **Data Quality Framework:** AASB S2 requires entities to prioritise **primary data** (sourced directly from value-chain activities) over secondary estimations, and to disclose information enabling users to understand the extent to which data is verified or primary.
* **Financed Emissions:** Additional disclosures are mandated for Category 15 (financed emissions) if an entity engages in asset management, commercial banking, or insurance activities.
* **Relief Mechanisms:** 
  * **1-Year Transition Relief:** Entities are not required to disclose Scope 3 emissions in their first year of applying AASB S2.
  * **Modified Liability:** Statements regarding Scope 3 emissions enjoy modified liability protections for reporting years commencing between 1 January 2025 and 31 December 2027.
